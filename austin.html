03 / Researcher

Background & Research.

Research focused on understanding the operational, technological and geopolitical dimensions of modern conflict.

Austin Burgess is the Founder and Chief Analyst of the Latitude Institute for Security Analysis (LISA). He studied at Medicine Hat College and has professional experience in private investigations, specializing in research, analysis and information gathering.

His work on the Russo-Ukrainian War led him to pursue further study in modern warfare, including the Understanding Modern Warfare online certificate programme through King's College London. His research has examined psychological warfare, disinformation, conflict financing and front-line logistics.

His primary research interests include military strategy, intelligence, information warfare and the wider geopolitical implications of armed conflict.
