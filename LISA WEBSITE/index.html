01 / Mission
LISA exists to make complex security issues understandable, accessible and useful.

We examine modern conflict, military strategy, defence policy, emerging technologies, intelligence and geopolitical developments through an independent Canadian lens.

01

Independent

Analysis developed independently from governments, political parties and institutional interests.

02

Evidence-Based

Claims are grounded in observable evidence, credible sources and transparent methodology.

03

Canadian Perspective

Global security developments are examined through the interests and responsibilities of Canada.

02 / Research

Areas of analysis.

LISA examines the security environment across multiple interconnected domains.

01

04 / Methodology

Analysis built on evidence.

LISA applies an open-source research methodology designed to distinguish verified information, analytical assessment and uncertainty.

01

Collection

Gathering information from publicly available and credible sources.

02

Verification

Cross-referencing information and evaluating the reliability of available evidence.

03

Analysis

Identifying patterns, relationships, capabilities, constraints and strategic implications.

04

Assessment

Presenting findings with clear distinctions between established facts, analysis and uncertainty.

05 / Researchers

The people behind the analysis.

LISA's research is conducted by a small team of researchers focused on understanding conflict, security and geopolitical developments through open-source evidence and independent analysis.

Austin Burgess

Chief Researcher · Ukraine

Austin Burgess is the Founder and Chief Analyst of the Latitude Institute for Security Analysis (LISA). After studying at Medicine Hat College, he gained professional experience in the private investigations field, specializing in research, analysis, and information gathering.

Austin founded LISA following his work as a researcher focused on the Russo-Ukrainian War. After achieving his Understanding Modern Warfare certificate from King's College London, he gained experience examining psychological warfare, disinformation, financial dimensions of the conflict, and front-line logistics. This deepened his interest in the intersection of conflict, intelligence, and national security, ultimately shaping the foundation of LISA.

His research focuses primarily on the Russo-Ukrainian War, with particular interests in military strategy, intelligence, information warfare, front-line logistics, and the broader geopolitical implications of the conflict.

PRIMARY FOCUS: MILITARY STRATEGY · INTELLIGENCE · INFORMATION WARFARE · FRONT-LINE LOGISTICS · GEOPOLITICS
austin@lisaorg.ca

Corina Solé

Chief Researcher · Middle East

Corina is LISA's primary researcher covering conflicts and security developments across the Middle East. She holds a Certificate in Peace Studies from the University of Saskatchewan and applies an interdisciplinary approach to understanding the causes, evolution, and future trajectories of modern conflicts.

Her primary area of focus is U.S. intervention and strategic involvement in Iran, while her broader research encompasses a range of lesser-known conflicts and security issues that have significant implications for North American, and especially Canadian interests.

Through her research, Corina seeks not only to understand the historical forces that shape conflict, but also to identify emerging trends and assess how they may influence the future of global security.

PRIMARY FOCUS: MIDDLE EAST · IRAN · CONFLICT ANALYSIS · U.S. STRATEGIC INVOLVEMENT · CANADIAN SECURITY
corina@lisaorg.ca
